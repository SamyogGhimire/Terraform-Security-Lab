Interpretation: with only 1-2 discordant cases between any pair of tools, McNemar's test has essentially no power at this sample size. None of the pairwise differences reported here should be read as statistically established; they describe this specific 20-case corpus, not a population of Terraform configurations. A corpus of several hundred independently-sourced cases would be needed before a significance claim is defensible.
