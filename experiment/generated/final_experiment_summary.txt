TERRAFORM MULTI-LAYER SECURITY VALIDATION EXPERIMENT
====================================================

DATASETS
----------------------------------------------------
Synthetic vulnerable cases : 10
Secure baseline cases      : 10
Public Terraform cases     : 5

CONTROLLED EVALUATION
----------------------------------------------------
The synthetic and secure baseline corpora are used
for TP, TN, FP, FN and classification metrics.

EXTERNAL VALIDATION
----------------------------------------------------
The public Terraform corpus is used to examine
real-world scanner findings, finding volume,
severity distribution and scanner complementarity.
